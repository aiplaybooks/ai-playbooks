Read by every writing agent (news/pack writer, hook writer, caption agent, repo writer, scouts) BEFORE writing.

## Current directives (2026-09-29)
- Our numbers (7 days): viral clip Reels are the only format that reaches people: IG 220 / YT 353 / FB 143 avg
  views; news and pack carousels get 0-2 views on IG/FB (1-3 followers: photo posts only reach followers).
- Carousels: their voiced video now also goes out as IG Reel + FB Reel. Write the carousel so the VIDEO works:
  a result-first cover (what the viewer gets), one idea per slide, voice-over lines that hook in the first 2 s.
  Captions must fit the Reel too (no "swipe", no "slide 3").
- Clips: the strongest format; the hook text on the frame must make the first second worth watching.
- Repo posts: the "Comment WORD → DM" CTA got 8 comments on the first repo post: keep it on every repo post.
