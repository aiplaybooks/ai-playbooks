AAI Playbooks

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can now read your bank statement and build a real budget from it. Here are 5 prompts to try:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Table Builder

Turn a messy bank statement into a clean income vs. expense table.

I'm uploading my bank statement as a [CSV/PDF]. Build a clean table with columns Date, Description, Category, and Amount. Add a summary at the top: total income, total expenses, and net savings for the period.

02

The Category Sorter

See exactly where your money actually goes.

Using the transaction table above, group every expense into categories like Rent, Groceries, Transport, Subscriptions, and Eating Out. Show each category's total and its percentage of my total spending, ranked from highest to lowest.

TIP Ask a follow-up like 'which category grew the most vs last month' if you have two statements.

03

The Savings Planner

A month-by-month plan for a goal you actually pick.

I want to save [AMOUNT] in [NUMBER] months for [GOAL]. Based on my income and expense totals above, suggest a realistic monthly savings target and which categories I could trim to hit it, without listing this as financial advice.

04

The Debt Payoff Order

A clear order to pay off what you owe.

Here are my debts: [LIST NAME, BALANCE, INTEREST RATE FOR EACH]. Suggest a payoff order using both the avalanche method (highest interest first) and the snowball method (smallest balance first), and explain the trade-off between the two for my situation. This is not financial advice.

TIP Bring this to a licensed advisor before making big decisions — use it to understand your options, not as final advice.

05

The Subscription Cleanup

Find every recurring charge hiding in your statement.

Scan the transaction table above for recurring charges (same amount, roughly monthly). List each one with its name, amount, and how many months it appeared. Flag any I might have forgotten I'm paying for.

Get more out of this pack

  • Export a real CSV — Most banking apps let you download a statement as CSV — it works better than a screenshot.
  • Run it monthly — Same five prompts, new statement — watch the categories change over time.
  • Keep it factual — Use the numbers to decide, not the AI's opinion — it's a calculator, not an advisor.
