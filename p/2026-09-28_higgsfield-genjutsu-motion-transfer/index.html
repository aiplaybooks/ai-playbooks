Two modes, one reference video

WHAT GENJUTSU DOES

  • Motion Transfer: Keeps the motion, camera work and timing, then rebuilds the scene around a new character
  • Object Swap: Changes one chosen element — a character, outfit, product or location — keeps the rest untouched
  • What carries over: Acting, lip sync, camera movement and VFX stay consistent between the original and the new version
  • No prompt needed: Pick a mode, upload your video, choose a preset or add a short description
