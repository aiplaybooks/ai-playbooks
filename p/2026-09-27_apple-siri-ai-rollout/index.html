Siri AI, in beta

What launched

  • Rollout: Began September 14, 2026, with iOS 27
  • Language: English first

What's actually different

3 things to try today

  • Visual Intelligence — Point your Camera at something and ask Siri about it
  • Follow-up questions — Keep asking without repeating yourself — Siri holds the context
  • Cross-app actions — Ask it to act inside apps like WhatsApp and Audible

Visual Intelligence, on iPhone

Try it now

  • Open the Camera app — Point it at whatever you want to ask about
  • Tap the Siri AI button — It appears right in the camera view
  • Ask a question — "What is this?" or "add this event to my calendar"
  • Keep going — Ask a follow-up — it remembers what you just asked

Onscreen awareness

It understands your screen

  • On a website: Ask it to find something on the page and add it to your calendar
  • In your messages: It can look up something you mentioned earlier and act on it
  • Works across: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro

Before you get too excited

Not everywhere yet

  • Not available in the European Union at launch

  • English only for now — 5 more languages land in October
