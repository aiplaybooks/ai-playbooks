WhatsApp calls on Instagram

What launched

  • What it does: Someone on your Instagram profile can tap to call you on WhatsApp
  • Where the number comes from: The phone number linked in your Meta Accounts Center
  • Default: Off, you turn it on yourself
  • Rollout: Gradually, over the coming months, not everyone has it yet

Who can call you

You stay in control

  • Everyone — Any Instagram profile can start a WhatsApp call to you
  • Your contacts — Only people saved in your phone's contacts
  • No one — Keep the feature fully off

Where to find it

Check your setting

  • Open Instagram settings — Go to Accounts Center from your profile
  • Tap your linked accounts — Find the WhatsApp connection
  • Open call permissions — Choose everyone, contacts, or no one
  • Save — The setting applies the next time someone visits your profile

What doesn't change

Still private

  • Encryption: WhatsApp calls stay end-to-end encrypted either way
  • Messages: Your WhatsApp chats aren't affected by this setting
  • Linking: You need WhatsApp added to the same Accounts Center as Instagram

Before you turn it on

Worth knowing

  • Set to "everyone" and any follower can call your number, not just message you
  • Requires WhatsApp linked in the same Accounts Center as your Instagram
  • Still rolling out, so it may not show up on your account yet
