AAI Playbooks

A faceless page plus one AI agent can realistically build toward $3,000 a month. 7 prompts to build it:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Niche Picker

Find a niche that pays before you post once.

Act as a strategist who has grown faceless pages. Give me 5 faceless page niches for [PLATFORM] that could realistically reach [$3,000] a month. For each: the audience, 3 ways it makes money, how crowded it is, and ONE PROOF that people already pay in that niche.

02

The Content Engine

30 days of posts from one brainstorm.

Be my content director for a faceless [NICHE] page. Build a 30-day plan as a table: 3 content pillars, the format for each day (carousel, Reel, story), a hook for every post, and which posts should send people to my [PRODUCT OR LINK].

TIP Feed it your best-performing post once you have data, so month two builds on what actually works.

03

The Agent Blueprint

What an AI agent can do for you every day.

Act as an automation architect. Design an AI agent workflow for my [NICHE] page using [n8n / Make / Zapier]: it finds trending topics, drafts captions in my voice, builds a posting schedule and sends me everything to APPROVE. List each step, the tool for it, and what I still do by hand.

04

The Hook Factory

Stop the scroll in the first line.

Write 20 scroll-stopping hooks about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Use 5 styles: a bold claim, a question, a number, a mistake, a before/after. Keep each under 12 words, nothing I can't back up, and mark the 3 TO TEST FIRST with the reason.

TIP Post the 3 marked hooks first and let real engagement decide the rest of the batch.

05

The Money Map

Every income stream, in the right order.

Act as a creator monetization coach. My faceless page has [FOLLOWERS] followers in [NICHE]. Map every realistic income stream (affiliates, sponsors, a digital product, UGC): what each needs, what it typically pays, and the ORDER to launch them to reach [$3,000] a month.

06

The Sponsor Pitch

Land your first brand deal.

Write a short sponsorship pitch to [BRAND] from my [NICHE] page with [FOLLOWERS] followers and [ENGAGEMENT RATE]% engagement. Include a one-line opener, why my audience fits their product, 3 package options with prices, and a polite follow-up for 5 days later.

TIP Send it from an email that matches your page name — it reads far more professional than a personal address.

07

The Growth Auditor

Find out why a post flopped.

Act as a growth analyst. Here are my last 10 posts with reach, saves and shares: [PASTE STATS]. Tell me what the top 3 have in common, what the weakest 3 did wrong, 3 experiments for next week, and a REALISTIC follower range for the next 30 days.

Get more out of this pack

  • Run one prompt at a time — Keep the niche pick, content plan and agent blueprint in the same chat so each step builds on the last.
  • Start the agent as a checklist — Before wiring up n8n or Zapier, run the workflow by hand for a week so you know what to automate first.
  • Review before you post — The agent drafts, you approve — never let it publish without a human reading it first.
