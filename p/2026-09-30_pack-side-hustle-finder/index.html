Turn 5 free hours a week into an extra $1,000 a month. 7 prompts to find your side hustle:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Skill Scanner

What you know that others pay for

Interview me to find my sellable skills. Ask me 8 questions, one at a time, about my job, hobbies, what friends ask me for help with, and tools I use. Then list 5 skills people pay for, with PROOF that a market exists for each.

TIP Answer honestly, even the small stuff — the skill you think is boring is usually the one people pay fastest for.

02

The Hustle Matcher

Ideas that fit your real life

Act as a career coach. My skills: [SKILLS]. I have [HOURS] hours a week, [$BUDGET] to start, and I prefer [ONLINE/OFFLINE]. Suggest 7 side hustles that fit, with startup cost, time to first income, a realistic monthly range, and the BIGGEST RISK of each.

TIP Give it your real hours, not your hoped-for hours — a hustle that needs 15 hours will just get abandoned.

03

The Reality Check

Kill bad ideas before they cost you

Be brutally honest about this side hustle idea: [IDEA]. What does a typical beginner earn in the first 3 months, what does it really cost in time, what do most people get wrong, and what would make you say DON'T DO IT?

TIP This one is built to disagree with you — that's the whole point of running it.

04

The 7-Day Test

Prove it with one small sale

Design a 7-day experiment to test [SIDE HUSTLE] with under [$BUDGET]: day-by-day tasks under 45 minutes, the smallest offer I can sell, where to find the first 3 customers, and the number that tells me to CONTINUE OR STOP.

05

The Offer Writer

Say what you sell in one sentence

Write my offer for [SIDE HUSTLE] for [AUDIENCE]: a one-sentence description, 3 package options with prices, what's included, the delivery time, and a short message I can send to 10 people I know TODAY.

TIP Send the message to 10 real people before you build anything else — replies are your fastest signal.

06

The Time Planner

Fit it around a full-time job

I work [WORK HOURS] and have these free slots: [FREE TIME]. Build a weekly schedule for my side hustle [SIDE HUSTLE] that avoids burnout: which tasks go in which slot, what to automate or batch, and ONE DAY OFF that stays off.

TIP Protect that one day off in the calendar first — everything else fits around it, not the other way.

07

The Scale Plan

From first sale to steady income

My side hustle [SIDE HUSTLE] made [$AMOUNT] last month from [CLIENTS] clients. What should I change to reach [$GOAL] a month without doubling my hours? Cover pricing, packaging, repeat clients, and what to STOP DOING.

TIP The "what to stop doing" line usually matters more than anything you'd add.
