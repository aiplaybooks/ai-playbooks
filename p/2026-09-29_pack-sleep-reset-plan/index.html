Your phone is wrecking your sleep. 6 AI prompts build you a real reset plan in 2 weeks:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Sleep Audit

Find out what's actually breaking your sleep

Act as a sleep coach. Here's my night: [DESCRIBE YOUR EVENING, BEDTIME, PHONE USE, CAFFEINE]. List the 3 biggest problems, rank them by impact, and give me ONE SMALL CHANGE to start tonight.

TIP Be honest about the phone-in-bed part — that's usually problem #1.

02

The Wind-Down Architect

A 45-minute routine that actually gets you sleepy

Build me a 45-minute wind-down routine before my target bedtime of [TIME]. Include a SCREEN CUTOFF, one calming activity, and a fallback version for nights I only have 15 minutes.

TIP Keep the fallback version — a broken streak kills the habit faster than a short routine.

03

The Screen-Time Contract

Rules you'll actually follow

Write me a screen-time contract for the hour before bed: which apps are BANNED after [TIME], what I'm allowed instead, and one honest consequence if I break it. Keep it realistic, not extreme.

TIP One realistic rule you keep beats three strict ones you quit by Wednesday.

04

The Bedroom Reset

Fix the room, not just the habit

Here's my bedroom setup: [LIGHT, NOISE, TEMPERATURE, WHAT'S ON YOUR NIGHTSTAND]. Give me a BEDROOM CHECKLIST to fix before my next reset attempt, cheapest fixes first.

TIP Ask for cheapest-first — most fixes here cost nothing.

05

The 2-Week Reset Calendar

Move your bedtime gradually, not all at once

I currently fall asleep at [CURRENT TIME] and want to fall asleep by [TARGET TIME]. Build me a 2-WEEK CALENDAR that shifts my bedtime gradually, day by day, with what to do if I miss a day.

06

The Tracking Sheet

See the reset actually working

Build me a simple daily sleep tracker template: bedtime, wake time, how rested I feel out of 10, and one note field. After 7 days, tell me what to ask you to ADJUST THE PLAN.

TIP One week of honest numbers beats a month of guessing how you slept.
