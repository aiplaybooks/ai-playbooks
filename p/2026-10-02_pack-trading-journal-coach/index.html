AAI Playbooks

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can read your trade journal and find your worst habit. 7 prompts to make them your trading coach:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Trade Plan

Rules before you click buy

Act as a trading coach. Help me write my trading plan for [MARKET] on the [TIMEFRAME] chart: which setups I take, entry and exit rules, max risk per trade, max trades per day, and the conditions where I DON'T TRADE at all.

TIP Write it down before your next session. Rules you can see beat rules you remember.

02

The Position Sizer

Never risk more than you planned

My account is [$ACCOUNT], I risk [RISK]% per trade. Entry [PRICE], stop loss [STOP], target [TARGET]. Calculate position size, the money at risk, the reward-to-risk ratio, and tell me if this trade FITS MY RULES: [RULES].

03

The Journal Analyst

Find the pattern in your losses

Here is my trade journal from the last month: [PASTE TRADES WITH DATE, SETUP, ENTRY, EXIT, RESULT, NOTES]. Find my win rate per setup, time of day, and emotion tag, and name the ONE HABIT that costs me the most.

TIP Paste even 10 trades. The pattern shows up faster than you'd think.

04

The Post-Mortem

Learn from one bad trade

Review this losing trade like a strict coach: [DESCRIBE TRADE AND WHAT I WAS THINKING]. Was it a bad trade or a good trade that lost? Which rule did I break, what did I ignore, and what is the LESSON in one sentence?

TIP Do this right after the loss, while it's still fresh.

05

The Tilt Check

Stop revenge trading

Before each session, ask me 6 short questions about sleep, mood, recent losses and why I want to trade today. Based on my answers, tell me to trade normally, reduce size, or SKIP THE DAY, and explain why.

TIP Answer honestly. It only works if you don't lie to it.

06

The Backtest Designer

Test the idea before real money

I have a trading idea: [DESCRIBE STRATEGY]. Turn it into exact, testable rules, design a manual backtest on [NUMBER] past examples, the numbers to record for each, and the results that would make me DROP THE IDEA.

TIP Test it on paper first. Real money comes after, not before.

07

The Weekly Review

One improvement per week

Here's my week: [TRADES, P/L, RULE BREAKS, NOTES]. Write my weekly review: what went well, what went wrong, whether I followed my plan (score 1-10), and ONE FOCUS for next week with a way to measure it.

TIP Same day, same time, every week. Consistency is the whole point.
