AAI Playbooks

ChatGPT and Claude can build you a workout and meal plan that actually fits your real week. Here are 5 prompts:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Weekly Split

A workout plan built around the days you actually have.

Build me a weekly workout plan using only the time I really have: [DAYS I CAN TRAIN], [MINUTES PER SESSION], [EQUIPMENT: HOME / GYM / NONE]. I'm at a [FITNESS LEVEL] level. Make it SPECIFIC (sets, reps, rest) and REALISTIC for a busy week, not an ideal one.

TIP Give it your worst week, not your best one — the plan should still work when Tuesday gets eaten by a late meeting.

02

The One-List Shop

Every ingredient from your meals, combined into one trip.

Take this week's meals: [LIST YOUR MEALS OR PASTE A PLAN]. Combine every ingredient into ONE grocery list, grouped by store aisle (produce, protein, pantry...), with quantities for [NUMBER OF PEOPLE]. The goal: no double trips, no guessing.

TIP Paste it straight into your phone's notes app — grouped by aisle, it doubles as a map of the store.

03

Meals For Your Constraints

Dinner ideas that fit your diet, budget and time.

Suggest 7 dinner ideas that fit [DIETARY CONSTRAINTS: e.g. vegetarian, no dairy], a weekly budget of [BUDGET], and under [MAX COOK TIME] minutes each. Prioritize meals that REUSE the same ingredients so nothing goes to waste.

TIP Ask it to reuse the SAME 8-10 ingredients across all 7 meals — that's what actually keeps the grocery bill down.

04

The Progress Tracker

One simple table that shows if it's actually working.

Build me a simple weekly tracker table for [WORKOUTS COMPLETED, WEIGHT OR MEASUREMENTS, ENERGY LEVEL 1-5]. Keep it to one table I can update in under a minute, and have it flag when a number hasn't MOVED in 2 weeks.

TIP Update it on the same day every week — Sunday night works well — so the trend is actually comparable.

05

The Simplify Button

For when the plan gets too big to keep up with.

My routine from the prompts above has gotten too complicated and I've skipped it [NUMBER] times this week. Cut it down to the SMALLEST version that still counts as progress, based on only [TIME OR DAYS I ACTUALLY HAVE] right now.

TIP Run this the moment you notice you're avoiding the plan — before you quit it completely.

Get more out of this pack

  • Keep it one thread — Run all 5 prompts in the same chat so it remembers your schedule, budget and level throughout.
  • Be exact about your week — Real days and real minutes beat 'sometime this week' — vague input gives vague plans.
  • Re-run it every Sunday — Feed last week's tracker numbers back into prompt one to adjust the plan, not just repeat it.
