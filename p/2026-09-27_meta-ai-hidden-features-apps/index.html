5 things Meta AI can already do

THE LIST

  • Restyle — Redraw a Story photo or clip from a text prompt
  • Imagine & edit — Generate images in a DM, then ask for changes
  • Ask anything — Type @Meta AI in a chat or use Search
  • Message Summaries — WhatsApp turns your unread chats into bullet points
  • Auto-reply — Businesses can let Meta AI answer customers

Restyle turns a photo into a whole new scene

01 · INSTAGRAM STORIES

  • Open a Story — Add a photo or video like you normally would
  • Tap Restyle — It sits at the top of the Stories editor
  • Type a prompt — "watercolor", "add a crown", "sunset background" — Meta AI redraws it

Generate and edit images without leaving the chat

02 · INSTAGRAM DMs

  • Type /imagine — or @Meta AI imagine plus what you want, in any DM
  • Pick a result — Meta AI replies inline with a set of images
  • Ask for a tweak — "add a hat", "make it night" — Imagine Edit updates it

Meta AI answers questions right where you're scrolling

03 · SEARCH & CHATS

  • Type @Meta AI — in any 1:1 or group DM, then ask your question
  • Or use Search — Instagram's search bar can ask Meta AI directly
  • Same window, same login — no separate app, no new account

Let Meta AI catch you up on unread chats

04 · WHATSAPP

  • Tap the unread count — on a chat with messages piling up
  • Get bullet points — a private summary of what you missed
  • Off by default — turn it on in Settings › Chats › Private Processing

Business pages can let Meta AI handle the replies

05 · BUSINESS ACCOUNTS

  • Meta Business Agent — answers customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger
  • Three modes — you write every reply, approve AI suggestions, or let it answer alone
  • It learns your business — from your Page, past chats and your website
