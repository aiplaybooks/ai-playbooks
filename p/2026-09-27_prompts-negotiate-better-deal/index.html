These 5 prompts can talk your rent, your bill, and your next car down by hundreds of dollars. Here's the script:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Rent Rescue

Turn a rent increase into a lower renewal offer.

Act as an expert negotiator. My landlord wants to raise my rent from [CURRENT RENT] to [NEW RENT] at [ADDRESS]. My leverage is [ON-TIME PAYMENTS / CHEAPER COMPARABLES / LONG TENANCY]. Write a POLITE COUNTER-OFFER, a fallback ask if they refuse, and a realistic estimate of what I could save.

TIP Send it in writing, not a phone call — a paper trail gives you room to negotiate again next year.

02

The Used Car Closer

Walk into the lot with a number and a script.

Act as an expert car negotiator. I'm buying a [YEAR/MAKE/MODEL] listed at [ASKING PRICE] with [MILEAGE/KNOWN ISSUES]. My leverage is [COMPARABLE LISTINGS / CASH OFFER / TIME ON THE LOT]. Give me a TARGET PRICE, an opening offer, a back-and-forth script, and a walk-away line.

TIP Get three comparable listings ready before you go — specific numbers beat 'it feels expensive.'

03

The Retention Call Script

Get a discount before you even threaten to cancel.

Act as an expert negotiator who worked in telecom retention. I want to lower my [INTERNET / PHONE / STREAMING] bill of [CURRENT PRICE] a month. My leverage is [COMPETITOR OFFER / YEARS AS A CUSTOMER]. Write a STEP-BY-STEP CALL SCRIPT ending in cancellation, plus a realistic estimate of the discount I could get.

TIP Ask to be transferred to the 'retention department' by name — they can offer deals regular support can't.

04

The Service Provider Reset

Cut a bill that just went up, without switching providers.

Act as an expert negotiator. My [INSURANCE / GYM / SOFTWARE] plan costs [CURRENT PRICE] and just went up. My leverage is [X YEARS AS A CUSTOMER / A CHEAPER COMPETITOR QUOTE]. Write a short, firm message asking for a RATE MATCH OR DISCOUNT, plus a fallback ask if they say no.

TIP Always ask 'is this the best you can do' one more time after their first answer — it often isn't.

05

The Contractor Counter

Push back on a quote without losing the job.

Act as an expert negotiator. A contractor quoted me [PRICE] for [JOB]. My leverage is [ANOTHER QUOTE / A FLEXIBLE TIMELINE]. Write a RESPECTFUL COUNTER asking for a lower price or added value, plus a realistic range of what I could save.

TIP Get a second quote first — it's usually free, and it doubles your leverage in the first message.

Get more out of this pack

  • Fill in real numbers — The more specific your leverage, the sharper the script — vague inputs get vague answers.
  • Practice it once out loud — Read the AI's script back to yourself before the real call so it sounds like you.
  • Always keep a walk-away line — The best negotiators are ready to leave — ask for one and actually mean it.
