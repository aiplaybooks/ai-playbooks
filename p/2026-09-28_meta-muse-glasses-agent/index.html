Not a voice assistant you describe things to

WHAT MUSE DOES

  • Look and ask: Say Muse's name and it acts on whatever you're looking at, no describing needed
  • Runs in the background: Muse keeps working on a task while you're still talking about something else
  • Fully hands-free: No phone, no typing — the whole interaction is voice plus your glasses' camera
  • Connected apps: New connectors this round: Walmart, Best Buy, Expedia, Instacart, Notion, GitHub

Three things Meta showed it doing

SEEING WHAT YOU SEE

  • Shop a shelf — Look at a product or a flier and ask Muse to check it or add it to your list
  • Check a flight — Ask about a price or a booking while Muse looks through your glasses with you
  • Book a table — Muse can act on a request in the background without breaking your conversation

How this differs from a normal voice assistant

  • You describe what you see out loud
  • One command, one answer, then it stops
  • Can't hold a task while you keep talking

Muse on glasses

How this differs from a normal voice assistant

  • It already sees what you're looking at
  • Keeps a long, in-depth conversation going
  • Finishes tasks in the background as you talk
