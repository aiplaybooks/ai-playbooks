"Comment GUIDE": the Instagram trick that turns comments into sales on autopilot. 7 prompts to build it:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Lead Magnet

A freebie people comment for.

Act as a funnel strategist. My audience is [AUDIENCE] and I sell [PRODUCT]. Give me 5 free lead magnet ideas people would comment a keyword to get (checklist, template, mini-guide), how fast I can make each, and which one leads most NATURALLY to my product.

02

The Keyword Post

A post built to earn comments.

Write an Instagram carousel outline that ends with 'Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [FREEBIE]'. 7 slides: a hook, 5 value slides that make people want more, and the ask. Add a caption whose first line repeats the KEYWORD offer.

TIP The value slides do the real work — a keyword ask with no value behind it just trains people to scroll past.

03

The DM Flow

An auto-reply that doesn't feel automatic.

Write the automated DM sequence for people who comment [KEYWORD]: the first message with the freebie, a question to learn what they need, 2 reply branches based on their answer, and a soft offer for [PRODUCT]. Friendly, short, and HUMAN.

04

The Tool Setup

Wire it up without code.

Explain step by step how to set up comment-to-DM automation for Instagram with [TOOL], using only official, policy-compliant features. Include the trigger, the message steps, how to tag leads, and the LIMITS or rules I must respect.

TIP Instagram only allows one automated private reply per comment, and it has to happen within 7 days — build the flow around that limit, not around what you wish it allowed.

05

The Follow-Up Sequence

For people who took the freebie and went quiet.

Write a 3-message follow-up for people who got my free [FREEBIE] but didn't buy [PRODUCT]: day 2 (a useful tip), day 4 (a short customer story), day 7 (a clear offer with a deadline). Each under 60 words, NO GUILT-TRIPPING.

06

The Objection DMs

Answers for 'how much?' and 'not now'.

People reply to my DMs about [PRODUCT] with: 'how much?', 'I'll think about it', 'is it worth it?', 'I don't have time'. Write a short, honest reply to each that answers the real worry and gives ONE next step.

TIP One next step per reply — three options in a DM reads as pressure, one reads as help.

07

The Funnel Review

Find the leak in your numbers.

Here are my funnel numbers: [COMMENTS] comments, [DMS] DMs sent, [REPLIES] replies, [SALES] sales. Tell me where the biggest leak is, 3 likely reasons, and one test for each stage I can run THIS WEEK.

Get more out of this pack

  • Start with one keyword — One clear keyword on one post beats three vague ones spread across your feed — easier to promote, easier to track.
  • Stay inside the platform's rules — Only use official automation features and respect the one-reply, 7-day window — that's what keeps the account safe.
  • Review the numbers weekly — Comments and DMs sent are vanity — replies and sales are the numbers that tell you where to fix the flow.
