AAI Playbooks

Brands will pay you $150-$500 per video, even with zero followers. 7 prompts to land your first UGC deal:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Portfolio Builder

3 sample videos before anyone pays you.

Act as a UGC coach. I have no clients yet. Plan 3 sample UGC videos I can film at home with products I already own in [CATEGORY]: the product, the angle, a 30-second script, and the shot list. Make each one show a different SKILL brands pay for.

02

The Brand Finder

Who is actually buying UGC right now.

Help me find brands that buy UGC in [CATEGORY]. Give me 10 types of brands to target, the signs a brand already pays for UGC (ads, creator posts, hiring pages), where to find their marketing contact, and which 5 to pitch first.

TIP Check the brand's own ad library before you pitch — if they're already running UGC-style ads, they're already paying for this.

03

The Script Writer

Ads that sound like a real person.

Write 3 UGC ad scripts for [PRODUCT] aimed at [AUDIENCE], 20-35 seconds each: a problem-solution, an unboxing, and a 'things I wish I knew' style. Add the first-3-second hook, what to show on screen, and the CALL TO ACTION for each.

04

The Pitch DM

A message brands actually answer.

Write a short cold email and a DM to [BRAND] offering UGC videos for [PRODUCT]. I'm new but have 3 samples: [LINK]. Keep it under 90 words, lead with an idea for their product, not with me, and end with ONE CLEAR question.

TIP Send the DM to the brand's own account and the email to their marketing address — send both, don't pick one.

05

The Rate Card

Price it so you don't work for free.

Act as a UGC business mentor. Build my rate card as a beginner in [COUNTRY]: price per video, a 3-video bundle, raw footage, usage rights for [30/90] days and whitelisting. Explain each line in one sentence so I can defend it on a call.

06

The Hook Variations

One video, five ad versions.

I filmed a UGC video for [PRODUCT]. Here is the script: [PASTE SCRIPT]. Write 5 alternative opening hooks I can record in 10 minutes so the brand can test them as separate ads, and tell me which hook fits a COLD AUDIENCE best.

TIP Brands pay extra for hook variations because it lets them run more ads from one shoot — mention this when you pitch it.

07

The Repeat Client

Turn one deal into a monthly retainer.

I delivered my first UGC videos to [BRAND]. Write the delivery message, a short results check-in for 2 weeks later, and a proposal for a monthly package of [NUMBER] videos, with 3 fresh content ideas that make saying YES easy.

Get more out of this pack

  • Film the 3 samples first — No brand signs a creator with zero videos to show — the portfolio builder comes before any pitch.
  • Pitch 5 brands, not 1 — Send the same pitch to all 5 brands from the brand finder the same week — most replies come from volume.
  • Never skip the rate card — Price it before the call, so you're not making up a number while a brand is waiting on the phone.
