AAI Playbooks

Build a digital product on Saturday, get your first sale on Sunday. 7 prompts that do the heavy lifting:

Thanks for following! Everything from the post, ready to copy. Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your details.

01

The Product Finder

Something people already search for.

Act as a digital product strategist. I'm good at [SKILL] and know [AUDIENCE]. Give me 7 digital product ideas I can finish in one weekend (templates, checklists, guides, prompt packs), the problem each solves, a fair price, and which one has the FASTEST FIRST SALE.

02

The Outline Maker

The full product plan in 5 minutes.

Create the complete outline for [PRODUCT IDEA] for [AUDIENCE]: sections, what goes into each, examples to include, and the one 'quick win' the buyer gets in the FIRST 10 MINUTES. Keep it small enough to finish in 2 days.

TIP If the outline has more than 6 sections, cut it — a smaller finished product beats a bigger unfinished one.

03

The Content Drafter

Write the product section by section.

Write section [NUMBER] of my product [PRODUCT NAME] based on this outline: [PASTE OUTLINE]. Practical, specific, with one example and one mini exercise. Plain language for [AUDIENCE], no padding, and flag anything I should CHECK myself.

04

The Sales Page

A page that explains and sells.

Write a short sales page for [PRODUCT NAME] at [$PRICE]: a headline, the problem, what's inside as bullet points, who it's for and NOT FOR, 3 honest FAQs, and a clear buy button text. No fake testimonials, no fake scarcity.

TIP Keep the 'who it's not for' line — it reads as honest, and it filters out refund requests later.

05

The Launch Posts

5 posts to announce it.

Write 5 social media posts to launch [PRODUCT NAME] on [PLATFORM]: a behind-the-scenes post, a problem post, a sneak peek, a 'who it's for' post, and a launch-day post. Give each a hook and a caption with ONE call to action.

06

The Price Tester

Don't guess the price.

Help me price [PRODUCT NAME] for [AUDIENCE]. Compare 3 price points ([$LOW], [$MID], [$HIGH]), what each signals, a bundle or upsell idea, and a simple way to test prices in the FIRST 2 WEEKS.

TIP Launch at the middle price — it's easier to add a discount later than to raise a price your first buyers saw.

07

The Feedback Loop

Turn buyers into better versions.

Write a short thank-you email for buyers of [PRODUCT NAME] with 3 questions that tell me what to improve, and plan version 2: what to add based on likely answers, and how to turn happy buyers into REVIEWS without pressure.

Get more out of this pack

  • Run it in order — Idea, then outline, then draft — each prompt feeds the next in one chat, so nothing gets rebuilt twice.
  • Pick the smallest idea — A template or checklist ships in a weekend; a full course doesn't. Small and finished beats big and stuck.
  • Sell before it's perfect — Launch with the outline and one strong section done — the sales page and the first buyers tell you what to polish.
