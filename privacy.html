Privacy Policy — AI Playbooks

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy covers the "AI Playbooks Publisher" app and the AI Playbooks Instagram account (@aiplaybooks.daily) and Facebook Page.

What the app does

The app is an internal publishing tool. It posts our own content (images and videos about AI tools) to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page through the official Meta APIs. It is not offered to other users and has no sign-up or login for the public.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of visitors or followers. The app only uses the access token of our own Page and Instagram account, which is stored securely and never shared.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data, there is nothing to delete. If you still have a request or a question, contact us by sending a direct message to @aiplaybooks.daily on Instagram or by opening an issue at github.com/aiplaybooks/ai-playbooks. We will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
